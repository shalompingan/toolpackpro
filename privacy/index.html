Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

AquaLog is made by ToolPack Pro ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information AquaLog collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Data you provide: tank names, tank size and type, water test readings (pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, temperature), water change, feeding, and cleaning logs, livestock profiles (species, nickname, date acquired), equipment and food records, and photos you add of your tanks or livestock.
  • Data collected automatically: crash reports, via the third-party SDK Sentry. We do not use Apple's App Analytics or any analytics/usage-tracking SDK, and Sentry's performance/session tracing is turned off (tracesSampleRate = 0) — Sentry only receives crash reports, not behavioral data.
  • Data we do NOT collect: no location data, no contacts, no advertising identifiers, and no usage analytics or behavioral tracking.

How we use it

To run AquaLog's core features (tank and water-parameter logging, calculators, and reminders), to sync your data across your own devices if you're a Pro subscriber, and to find and fix bugs from crash reports.

Storage

On the free tier, your data is stored only on your device, in a local SwiftData database — it is never uploaded anywhere. If you upgrade to Pro (subscription or one-time purchase), your data is additionally synced through your own private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit private database), so it stays in sync across your own devices. This iCloud data stays within your private CloudKit database — ToolPack Pro cannot see or access it. Crash reports are sent to Sentry's servers (see below).

Third parties

AquaLog uses Sentry for crash reporting only (performance and session tracking is disabled). Subscription and one-time purchases are processed by Apple's StoreKit; we don't receive your payment details. We do not use any advertising or analytics SDKs.

Your choices

Free-tier data lives only on your device — deleting the app deletes your data. Pro-tier data lives in your own private iCloud account and can be removed from there or by deleting your data in-app. You can also request deletion of any data associated with your use of AquaLog by emailing support@toolpackpro.com with your account details.

Children's privacy

AquaLog is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes. Continued use of an app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
