Tallyhour

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Tallyhour is a time tracker for freelancers who bill by the hour. It's made by ToolPack Pro ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

We don't collect your data

Tallyhour doesn't collect any data. It has no accounts, and we run no servers for it. Nothing you enter is uploaded to us or to any third party, so we can't see your clients, your hours or your rates.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage:

  • clients, with their names, hourly rates and colors
  • time entries, with start and end times, notes, and any entries you add or edit by hand
  • your settings, such as currency, week start, rounding and timer reminders

Tallyhour doesn't sync between devices, so data on your iPhone and data on your iPad are kept separately.

Device backup

If iCloud Backup is turned on for your device, this data is included in your device backup and stored by Apple, so you can restore it on a new device. This is Apple's device backup, not a sync feature of the app. We can't access your backups.

If you delete the app, or your device is lost or damaged without a backup, the data can't be recovered. Because we never receive a copy, we can't restore it for you.

Timer reminders

If a timer keeps running for a long time, Tallyhour can remind you. These reminders are local notifications, scheduled on your device by iOS. They don't pass through any server. You can turn them off in the app's settings or in the iOS Settings app.

Exported files

Pro subscribers can export entries as PDF and CSV files. Exports open the iOS share sheet, and you decide where each file is saved and who receives it. A file leaves your device only when you choose to save or send it somewhere.

Subscription and payment

Subscriptions and payments are handled by Apple's App Store using StoreKit. The app only learns from the system whether your subscription is active and when it ends. It never receives your payment method or Apple Account details. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

Tallyhour is built only with Apple's own frameworks. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking code, and it makes no network requests to upload your content. It doesn't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share personal data.

Deleting your data

You can delete any client or entry inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from that device. Copies inside your device backups are managed by Apple in the iOS Settings app. There is nothing to delete on our side, because we never had the data.

Children's privacy

Tallyhour is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
