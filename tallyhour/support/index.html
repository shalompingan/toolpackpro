Restore your subscription

Reinstalled Tallyhour, or moved to a new iPhone or iPad? Your subscription belongs to your Apple Account, so you can get it back:

  1. Make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.
  2. Open Tallyhour and go to Settings.
  3. In the Subscription section, tap Restore Purchases.

Restoring brings back the subscription only. Your clients and entries come back from a device backup (see the next question).

Move your data to a new device

Tallyhour keeps your data only on your device and doesn't sync it, so it moves with your iCloud device backup:

  1. On your old device, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud › iCloud Backup, and tap Back Up Now.
  2. When you set up the new device, choose to restore from that iCloud backup.
  3. Open Tallyhour on the new device. Your clients and entries will be there; tap Restore Purchases if you subscribe to Pro.

If you delete the app, or a device is lost without a backup, the data can't be recovered. We never receive a copy, so we can't restore it for you. We recommend keeping iCloud Backup turned on.

Export your entries

Exporting PDF and CSV files is part of Tallyhour Pro. There are two places to export:

  1. Summary tab: tap the share button at the top right to export the period you're looking at.
  2. Settings › Export entries (CSV / PDF): pick a range, such as this month, last month, this year or all entries.

The file opens in the iOS share sheet, so you can save it to Files or send it to anyone you choose.

Cancel your subscription

You can cancel anytime, in either place:

  1. In Tallyhour: Settings › Manage Subscription.
  2. In the iOS Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the time you've paid for. After that you're back on the free plan: all your entries are kept, and your first 2 clients keep working. Refunds are handled by Apple.
