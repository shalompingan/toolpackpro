These terms cover your use of Tallyhour, a time tracker made by ToolPack Pro ("we", "us"), including the Tallyhour Pro subscription. Your use of the app is also covered by Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement. These terms add the details that are specific to Tallyhour.

Free plan

The free plan lets you use the first 2 clients you add, with no time limit on tracking or entries. You can start and stop timers, add and edit entries by hand, and see weekly, two-week and monthly summaries for those clients.

Tallyhour Pro

A Tallyhour Pro subscription adds:

  • unlimited clients
  • PDF and CSV export

Pro is offered as an auto-renewing subscription at $4.99 per month or $29.99 per year (US prices; the App Store shows the price in your local currency before you subscribe). Your first subscription includes a 7-day free trial. The trial length and the price after it are also shown on the subscription screen in the app.

Payment and renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple Account through the App Store when the free trial ends, or when you confirm the purchase if no trial applies. When the trial or the current period ends, the subscription renews and you are charged again, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

Cancelling

You can cancel anytime:

  • in the app, under Settings › Manage Subscription, or
  • in the iOS Settings app, under Apple Account › Subscriptions.

After you cancel, you keep access for the time you've already paid for. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple under its own policies, so refund requests go to Apple.

After your subscription ends

When your subscription ends, you go back to the free plan. All your entries are kept and you can always view them. The first 2 clients you added can still be tracked and edited; entries for other clients become view-only. Exporting requires a subscription again.

You keep your own data

Your data is stored only on your device (see the Privacy Policy). If you delete the app, or your device is lost or damaged without a backup, the data can't be recovered, and we have no copy to restore. We recommend keeping iCloud Backup turned on.

Amounts are for your records

The app calculates amounts from the hourly rates you enter and the time you record. They are for record keeping and reconciliation, and are not tax, legal or financial advice. Please check the figures before you send an invoice.
