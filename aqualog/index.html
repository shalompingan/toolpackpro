AquaLog

If you keep more than one tank, this is for you. AquaLog logs feeding and water tests for each tank, and reminds you when something is due.

Download on the App Store
iPhone & iPad

Every tank on one screen

The first screen lists your tanks. Each one shows its latest pH and ammonia, when you last tested it, and when the next water change is due.

Is the pH drifting?

Trend charts plot each reading over time and show the latest, lowest and highest value. There are eleven of them, from pH and ammonia to calcium, magnesium and phosphate.

Who lives in there

Keep a list of the fish in each tank, with the species, a nickname and a photo.

Water change in 4 days

Set how often a tank needs a water change, a feeding or a cleaning. AquaLog shows what is due next and reminds you when it is.

On iPad

The tank list stays on the left while you look at one tank on the right: its last water test, fish, maintenance log, reminders, equipment and food.
