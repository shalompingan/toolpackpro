Common questions

Why does Textproof need a shortcut?

iPhone lets apps read Messages only through Apple's Shortcuts app. Textproof's setup adds a shortcut called Textproof Reader, which reads the dates you pick and hands the messages straight to Textproof on your iPhone. Please keep its name as it is; Textproof looks for it by name.

Why does iPhone keep asking for permission?

During the test in setup, iPhone asks about 4 times whether the shortcut may run and share your messages with Textproof. Tap Always Allow each time. The first time you read a long period, it may ask once more. After that, it stops asking.

The test asks for up to 500 messages from the past year on purpose, so iPhone asks all its questions at once instead of again later.

It says it couldn't read my messages

One of the questions was probably answered with Don't Allow. Open the Shortcuts app, touch and hold Textproof Reader, tap Details › Privacy, allow Messages, then try again. If that doesn't help, delete the shortcut and add it again from Textproof's setup.

A message seems to be missing

  • A text you sent recently with a phone number or email address. iPhone can take up to a day before it makes these available to Shortcuts. Read again the next day.
  • It's outside the dates you picked. Start a new read with a wider range.
  • It was deleted from Messages before you read, so it isn't on your iPhone anymore.

When a reaction points to a message that isn't there, Textproof marks the spot as "A message may be missing here", both while you review and in the PDF.

Can I change what a message says?

No. You can change the two names shown in the PDF, hide whole messages, or cover words such as a phone number. The PDF lists what you hid or covered, and message text and times can't be edited. That's what makes the record trustworthy.

What is the fingerprint on the PDF?

It's a SHA-256 value calculated from the number, time, sender and text of every entry in the record. If anyone asks whether the file was changed, recalculating it from the same export gives the same result.

How do I restore my purchase?

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you paid with. Textproof checks your plan by itself when it opens; if it doesn't find it, tap Restore Purchases on the payment screen. Purchases are handled by Apple, so there's no Textproof account to sign in to.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On Textproof's home screen, tap Manage next to your plan. Or open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You keep access until the current period ends. The 7-day pass never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

How do I delete my data?

Starting a new read replaces the messages from the last one. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored, including exported PDFs you haven't shared or saved elsewhere. It doesn't delete anything from the Messages app.
