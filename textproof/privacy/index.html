Textproof

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Textproof is made by ToolPack Pro ("we", "us"). It turns a text conversation on your iPhone into a PDF you can hand to a lawyer, a court or HR. This policy explains what information Textproof handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

Information Textproof handles

  • Messages you choose to read: Textproof reads messages through Apple's Shortcuts app, using a shortcut called Textproof Reader that you add yourself. It reads only when you start a read, and only for the dates you pick. For each message it receives the text, the date and time, who sent it (the name, phone number or email address shown in Messages), and whether it was a text message or an iMessage. Photos and other attachments are not read.
  • What you add: the names you type for the PDF, and which messages you hide or which words you cover.
  • Data we don't collect: Textproof has no accounts and we run no server of our own. Your messages and contacts never reach us. The app shows no ads, contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. We don't sell or share personal data.

How we use it

Messages are used only inside the app, on your iPhone: to show them for review, and to make the PDF you export.

Storage

Messages that have been read, and your hide and cover choices, are stored only inside Textproof on your iPhone, with iPhone data protection, until you start a new read or delete the app. PDFs you export are kept in the app until you delete the app.

Textproof doesn't sync any of this to iCloud or send it anywhere. Like other app data, it's included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have them turned on.

Sharing

A PDF leaves your iPhone only when you tap Share, Save to Files or Print and choose where it goes. Email links open your own email app; nothing is sent until you send it.

Purchases

Reading, reviewing and previewing the first two pages are free. Exporting a full PDF needs one of these, all processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit: a weekly subscription, a 7-day pass that ends on its own and never renews, or a yearly subscription. We never receive your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether you have an active plan.

The weekly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can cancel at any time in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or on Textproof’s home screen by tapping Manage next to your plan.

Third parties

Textproof uses Apple for the Shortcuts app (to read messages on your iPhone) and for App Store purchases. We do not use any advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and the app makes no other network requests. Links in the app (adding the shortcut, the terms of use) open Apple's pages.

Your choices

Textproof can only read messages through the Textproof Reader shortcut. To stop it, delete the shortcut in the Shortcuts app, or change what it's allowed to share there under the shortcut's Details → Privacy. Deleting Textproof deletes everything it has stored on that iPhone, including exported PDFs that you haven't shared or saved elsewhere. Because your data never reaches us, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children's privacy

Textproof is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes. Continued use of an app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
