How to Use the Drywall Calculator

1

Measure Room

Measure the length, width, and height of your room in feet. Standard ceilings are 8 feet high.

2

Choose Sheet Size

Select your drywall sheet size. Standard 4×8 sheets work for most rooms. 4×12 reduces seams on long walls.

3

Add Openings

Enter the number of doors and windows. Each door subtracts 20 sq ft and each window subtracts 15 sq ft.

4

Get Count

See the exact number of sheets, square footage breakdown, and total material cost for your project.
