What AquaLog does

Everything a hobbyist actually needs, nothing an aquarium doesn't.

Water Parameter Log

Record pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate and more over time, and spot trends before they become problems.

Water Change Tracker

Calculate the right change ratio for your tank size and get reminders so changes never slip.

Dosing Calculator

Get the right dose for conditioners, fertilizers, and treatments based on your exact tank volume.

Feeding Schedule

Set feeding reminders per tank so every fish gets fed on schedule, even on your busy days.

Built for hobbyists, by ToolPack Pro

AquaLog is the first app from ToolPack Pro, a studio building small, focused iOS tools. We started with the aquarium community because good tank-keeping is mostly good record-keeping — and nothing simple existed to do it well.
