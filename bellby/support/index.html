Common questions

Will Bellby ring when my phone is on silent?

Yes. Bellby uses iPhone's own alarm system, so it rings on silent and in a Focus mode, even if Bellby isn't open. It needs the Alarms permission for this. To check, open the Settings app, tap Apps, then Bellby, and make sure Alarms is on.

Why does it keep ringing?

That's the point of Bellby. The alarm stops for good only when you finish the mission. If you leave the mission unfinished, it rings again every minute.

I can't walk or do squats today

About 20 seconds into a Walk or Squats mission, a small "Can't do this today?" link appears at the bottom. Tap it to switch to math instead. You'll get a couple of extra problems, and they'll be harder. If you know you'll need a break for a few days, change the alarm's mission to Math the night before.

My squats aren't being counted

  • Lean your iPhone against something so the front camera sees your whole body, from your hips down to your feet. Step back until the dashed outline disappears.
  • Turn on a light if the room is dark.
  • Squat down until your knees are clearly bent, then stand all the way up. That counts as one.

If the camera is turned off for Bellby, the mission offers math instead. To turn it back on, open the Settings app, tap Apps, then Bellby, and turn on Camera.

My steps aren't being counted

Walk needs Motion & Fitness access. Open the Settings app, tap Privacy & Security, then Motion & Fitness, and turn on Bellby. Steps can take a few seconds to show up, and they still count while the screen is off or the phone is in your pocket.

What is Wake-up Check?

With Wake-up Check on, the alarm rings once more 5 minutes after you finish the mission, in case you fell back asleep. Tap I'm Awake to end it. It's part of Bellby Pro and can be turned on for each alarm.

What do I get with Bellby Pro?

Math missions are free, forever. Pro adds the Walk and Squats missions and Wake-up Check. You can choose a yearly plan with a 7-day free trial, a monthly plan, or pay once for lifetime access.

How do I restore my purchase?

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you paid with. Bellby checks your purchase by itself when it opens; if it doesn't find it, open Bellby's Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are handled by Apple, so there's no Bellby account to sign in to.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Bellby, open Settings and tap the Bellby Pro card. Or open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You keep Pro until the current period ends. If you cancel at least 24 hours before the free week ends, you won't be charged. Lifetime is a one-time purchase, so there's nothing to cancel.

If Pro ends, alarms set to Walk or Squats give you math instead, so they still work. Your Walk and Squats settings come back if you subscribe again.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting an alarm removes it. Deleting the app removes everything Bellby stored, including your wake-up history.
