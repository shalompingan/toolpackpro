Bellby

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Bellby is an alarm clock that makes you finish a short mission, like a few math problems, before it stops ringing. It is made by ToolPack Pro ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Bellby does not collect any data. Everything you set up stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, Bellby saves the following on your device only:

  • Your alarms: time, repeat days, label, mission settings, sound and whether Wake-up Check is on.
  • Your wake-up history: when an alarm rang, when you finished the mission, how many problems, steps or squats you did and how many times it rang again. This is used for the summary you see after each mission.

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. It is removed when you delete Bellby. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or your computer, it may be included in that backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No third-party services or SDKs.
  • Bellby has no server. The only connection it makes is to Apple's App Store, to check Bellby Pro.

Permissions

Bellby asks for permission to schedule Alarms, so it can ring when your phone is on silent or in a Focus mode.

Two missions need one more permission, asked only when you choose them:

  • Walk uses Motion & Fitness to count your steps. Bellby only reads the step count while the mission is open.
  • Squats uses the Camera. Bellby looks at the live picture on your iPhone to count squats. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.

You can turn any of these off at any time in the Settings app. Bellby does not ask for your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Purchases

Bellby Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; Bellby never sees your payment details. Bellby only asks the App Store whether this Apple Account owns Pro.

Pro comes as a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, a monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can cancel at any time in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

Children

Bellby is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Bellby ever starts collecting data, this page will say exactly what and why before the update is released.
